Questions

Do these work in Google Sheets? Yes. Upload the .xlsx to Google Drive, then open it with Google Sheets. Excel and Numbers users just open the file.

Do I need to know formulas? No. You only type in the yellow cells and the log tabs. Totals, dashboards, statuses and progress bars fill in by themselves.

How were they checked? Every .xlsx template's formulas were checked by opening it in Apple Numbers and comparing the results with an independent calculation of the sample data.

Is this tax or financial advice? No. Tax set-asides, fees and payoff dates are estimates from the numbers you enter.
