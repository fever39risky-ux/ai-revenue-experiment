# Social Live Commentary Protocol

## Purpose
The social stream exists to document the experiment in public while the experiment is happening. It is secondary to revenue execution: posting must not materially delay actions that can generate revenue.

## Official period
- Preparation: 2026-08-26 through 2026-08-31
- Official 30-day verification: 2026-09-01 through 2026-09-30 (Asia/Tokyo)

## Primary channel
X is the primary live-commentary channel. Other channels may be used when the AI judges them worthwhile.

## What to post
Prefer event-driven updates rather than filler. Useful events include:
- a new revenue lane starts or stops
- a product is created, repriced, published, or withdrawn
- a distribution experiment launches
- a meaningful click, signup, inquiry, checkout, sale, affiliate conversion, refund, or failure occurs
- the AI changes strategy or reallocates effort
- a blocker is discovered or removed
- a new subagent, skill, connector, script, or automation is created because it changes execution capability
- daily close: concise summary + link to that day's Pages report

## Tone
Report what actually happened. Do not manufacture drama, inflate revenue potential, imply guaranteed earnings, or hide failures. Distinguish observed facts from hypotheses.

## Human role
Do not turn the owner into a copy-paste operator. If posting cannot be performed autonomously, first investigate API, Connector, MCP, browser automation, GitHub Actions, or another compliant automation path. Ask the owner only for one-time identity/OAuth/permission setup that cannot be self-granted.

## Logging
Important social posts should be referenced in the day's report and/or EVENTS.jsonl when they correspond to a meaningful experiment event.

## Reporting cadence
There is no fixed post count. The AI decides when an update is useful. The requirement is to maintain useful live commentary without optimizing for posting volume at the expense of revenue generation.
