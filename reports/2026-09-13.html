02 / DECISION

重要な判断と理由

  • Xを能動的な到達/集客レバーから撤退：2投稿・形式変更でも到達≈4-5＝アカウント自体の到達がほぼ0。希少なXクレジットを到達≈5の投稿に使うのは負EV。可逆（削除せず、到達回復やオーナー要請で再開可能）。
  • 残る非X到達レバーは所有日本語ページの検索流入のみ（種まき済み、索引化に時間）。供給の追加増設は需要未検証のため見送り（中間レビューの教訓）。
  • 本日はX投稿せず＝『まだ到達ゼロ/¥0』は読者価値なく、到達≈5で負EV。
  • カデンス：一時的2シフトを撤回。根拠（シグナル非依存バックログ＋~24hの速いXフィードバック）が消失。SEOは週単位で成熟するため日次判断のEVは低い→定時Routineを~2-3日毎へ縮小を推奨。
  • 失敗チャネルは休暇でなく方向転換：撤退したX分の労力をSEO待ちでなくnote.comへ再配分。代替（Etsy編集はブロック、円建てリンクはブロック、高単価はリーチ無しでは供給過多、Gumroadアフィリは近期弱）よりnote.comを選択＝実ドメイン権威を持つ最速の無料JP到達。
  • Retire X as the active reach/acquisition lever: two posts with varied format both reached ~4-5 = the account's own reach is near-zero; spending scarce X credits on ~5-reach posts is negative-EV. Reversible (nothing deleted; resumable on reach recovery or owner request).
  • The only remaining non-X reach lever is organic search on the owned JP pages (seeded; needs indexing time). No more speculative supply -- demand is unverified (midpoint-review lesson).
  • No X post today: 'still zero reach / ¥0' is not reader-worthy and reaches ~5 (negative-EV).
  • Cadence: revert the temporary two-shift posture -- its justification (a signal-independent backlog + a fast ~24h X feedback loop) is gone. SEO matures on a week-scale clock, so daily judgment has low EV -> recommend reducing the scheduled Routine to ~every 2-3 days.
  • A failed channel is a pivot, not a vacation: reallocated retired-X effort to note.com rather than waiting on slow SEO. note.com chosen over alternatives (Etsy edit blocked; JPY link blocked; higher-ticket = more supply w/o reach; Gumroad affiliate = weaker near-term) because it is the fastest free JP-audience reach with real domain authority.
