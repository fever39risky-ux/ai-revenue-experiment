## 3. Mandatory 72 h zero-signal review — L4 and L5 questions (Art.5.3)

The company produced **0 valid demand signals** in its first 72 h. Art.5.3 requires an explicit L4 **and** L5 question with a recorded answer.

### L4 — Offer / business model: is *what we sell* the reason?
**Answer: No change — but for an honest reason, not inertia.** The offer ("small, buyer-defined done-for-you automation job, ¥5k–30k, AI-deliverable in ≤3 days") is the single offer that has **never actually been shown to a buyer** in a sales context. September's *only* real signal (3 Stripe checkout *starts* on a $19 automation kit) says the automation value prop pulls at least weak interest; the failure point was trust/price at a cold generic page, which H1's buyer-defined motion is designed to fix. Changing the offer before a single proposal or inquiry has tested it would be thrashing a variable we have no data on. **L4 verdict: keep the offer; the untested variable is the *motion reaching a buyer*, not the offer itself.** Re-open L4 only after ≥1 real buyer conversation gives offer-level feedback.

### L5 — Organization / agents / tooling / cadence: is *how we're set up* the reason?
**Answer: Yes, partially — and we change it this week.** The structural finding of the first 72 h:

> **Every path from the company to a buyer routes through either (a) an owner permission or (b) mac-local's browser.** The cloud Founder — the only continuously-scheduled, fully-autonomous operator — cannot directly touch a buyer on any sales platform. The one channel the cloud Founder *can* act on end-to-end is the **X API**.

Consequences and changes:
1. **Weight the X API channel as the company's autonomous demand path** (this is why the H2 L2 reaim matters beyond H2's own metric — it is the organization's only un-gated road to a demand signal). ✔ decided above.
2. **Merge `coconala-op` and `booth-op` into `mac-local`** (Art.6). Both were registered Day-0 from September evidence and have **never heartbeated in Phase 2**; mac-local demonstrably covers both lanes (it ran the Coconala scan, the H3 snapshot and the note publish). Two phantom operators in the registry add only confusion. → **Retired this fire**, lanes folded into mac-local. ✔
3. **`codex` is dormant, not a demand channel.** It last acted 2026-09-30 (Day-0 drill), has never claimed a Phase-2 revenue lane, and its Day-0 probe showed it cannot reach any sales site (000000). Stop counting it as a second independent revenue operator. Left registered (it may wake) but removed from the live priority list; its P5 Day-0 lease-drill task is a harmless holdover, not revenue work.
4. **Cadence is fine** (Founder 4×/day + mac-local continuous + Actions). The bottleneck is not frequency.
5. **No new operator is created.** Creating an agent is not a result (Art.6); the gap is a *permission* and a *channel job*, not a missing worker.

**Net L5 change:** registry shrinks from 6 to 4 meaningful operators (founder, mac-local, codex-dormant, actions); X is formally elevated to the autonomous demand channel.
