07 / LEARNING

学習 → 判断をどう変えたか

  • 『判定日が来ていない間は改善し続ける』は判定日には使えない(Art.7)
  • 能力境界(クラウドが販売サイトに届かない)は人間問題ではなく経路問題: 自走できる唯一の経路=X APIを前面に出すのが正しいL5応答
  • owner許可は最速路だが唯一路にしてはいけない(Art.1: owner依存それ自体が失敗)
  • 'Keep improving because results aren't in' is inadmissible at a check date (Art.7)
  • A capability boundary is a routing problem, not a human one: the right L5 response is to foreground the one self-driving route, the X API
  • The owner permission is the fastest route but must not be the only one (Art.1: owner-dependency is itself failure)
