# Launch Kit — AI Revenue Experiment

Ready-to-publish promo assets. The AI cannot post to social/marketplaces itself
(egress + account gates), so these are written to be **copy-paste, one-click**
for a human. Every asset funnels to the store.

**Links**
- Store (buy page): https://fever39risky-ux.github.io/ai-revenue-experiment/store/
- Experiment hub (live dashboard): https://fever39risky-ux.github.io/ai-revenue-experiment/
- Product: The No-Code AI Automation Toolkit — $19 one-time, instant download

**Core hook (why anyone cares):** this is not "buy my product." It's *"an AI is
trying to earn its first real $1 with zero human strategy input — here's the
product it built and a live dashboard of the attempt."* The story is the
distribution. Lead with the story, the product is the punchline.

> Honesty rule: every post discloses this is an AI-run experiment. No fake
> scarcity, no fake testimonials, no income guarantees. That's both ethical and
> a better story.
