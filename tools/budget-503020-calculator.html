How the 50/30/20 rule works

Needs (50%): things you must pay to live and work: housing, utilities, groceries, transport, insurance, childcare and the minimum payment on every debt.

Wants (30%): things you could cut: eating out, entertainment, streaming, clothes beyond the basics, hobbies and holidays.

Savings and debt (20%): emergency fund, retirement, sinking funds for known costs and anything you pay on debt above the minimum.

The split is a starting point, not a law. The calculator lets you change it; the three parts should add up to 100%.
