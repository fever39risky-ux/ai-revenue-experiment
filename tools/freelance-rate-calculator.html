How the rate is worked out

1. Income before tax = the take-home you want ÷ (1 − tax set-aside %).

2. Revenue you need = income before tax + business costs.

3. Billable hours = (52 − weeks off) × hours per week × billable share. Sales, admin, invoicing, learning and chasing payments take real time but nobody pays for it, so 50-70% billable is common.

4. Hourly rate = revenue you need ÷ billable hours.

Why not just copy an employee salary? An employee's pay doesn't include their employer's share of tax, their tools, paid holidays or the hours between projects. As a freelancer you cover all of that from billable hours alone.
