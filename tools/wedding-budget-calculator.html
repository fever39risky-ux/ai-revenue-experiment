How to split a wedding budget

Venue and catering are the big two. Together they are often around half of the total, and catering grows with every guest. That is why the guest count is the fastest way to change your budget.

Cost per guest is the total budget divided by guests. The guests that fit figure uses your catering share and the per-guest quote from your caterer: catering budget ÷ price per guest.

Keep a buffer. The "Other" line is your buffer for overtime, tips, alterations, fees and surprises. 4 to 10% is sensible.

Monthly saving is what you still need (budget minus already saved) divided by the months left. Deposits are often due months earlier, so check vendor due dates too.
